# Oralyn — Organic discovery and distribution plan

## Positioning

**Working positioning:** The connected dental-practice platform for patient access and clinic-team workflows.

**Primary audience:** Independent dental clinics and small multi-chair practices in India.
**Secondary audience:** Dental clinic owners, practice managers and front-desk teams.

Keep the product story accurate. Oralyn is in development; do not publish customer counts, ratings, uptime figures, security certifications, integrations, clinical outcomes or testimonials unless independently verified and approved.

## Website structure to build

The first public-facing product page is at `/oralyn/`. Keep it separate from the clinic application routes.

Create these focused pages next, only when the corresponding feature and copy are verified:

1. `/oralyn/dental-clinic-management-software/` — overview of the clinic workflow.
2. `/oralyn/dental-appointment-management/` — patient requests and clinic coordination.
3. `/oralyn/dental-patient-records/` — records and access controls; describe only shipped functionality.
4. `/oralyn/dental-clinic-assistant-workflow/` — assistant role and permission boundaries.
5. `/oralyn/doctor-workspace/` — doctor-facing workflow; clinical tools are doctor-only.
6. `/oralyn/security-and-privacy/` — explain actual safeguards, hosting, access control, retention and incident handling only after technical verification.
7. `/oralyn/resources/` — educational resources for running a modern dental practice.
8. `/oralyn/contact/` — real business contact details and an enquiry path.

Do not create thin doorway pages that repeat the same paragraph with different city names. Do not claim Oralyn is a clinic in every city.

## Search foundations

Before enabling indexing on the final public domain:

- Confirm the production domain and HTTPS.
- Remove the staging-only `noindex,nofollow` directive from the marketing page only after the production domain is attached.
- Keep the application login and private workspace routes out of search results.
- Publish a canonical URL, XML sitemap and robots.txt for the final domain.
- Add valid Organization and SoftwareApplication structured data; use only verified brand, pricing, availability and contact details.
- Set unique page titles, descriptions, one clear H1, descriptive internal links and useful image alt text.
- Connect Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools; submit the sitemap.
- Check indexing, mobile usability, Core Web Vitals, broken links and structured-data validation.
- Add analytics only with an appropriate privacy notice and consent configuration.

## Free discovery and distribution channels

Create official profiles with consistent brand name, logo, description and the final domain:

- LinkedIn company page — product updates, founder notes, workflow demonstrations.
- YouTube channel — short product walkthroughs, feature explainers and dental-practice workflow education.
- Instagram and Facebook — visual demos, release notes and practical clinic operations tips.
- Relevant Indian dental communities and professional groups — contribute useful answers; do not spam or impersonate dentists.
- GitHub public project or changelog only if the product architecture and repository are intentionally public.

After the product is stable and has real users, evaluate software directories such as Capterra, G2, GetApp, SoftwareSuggest and Product Hunt. Complete listings honestly; do not buy fake reviews or invent customer references. Check current submission terms and costs before listing.

Google Business Profile is for an eligible real-world business location, not a substitute for ranking a software product website. Do not create a fake office listing.

## Content plan

Publish useful, original material based on actual product capability and real clinic workflow knowledge:

- How a dental clinic can organise appointment requests.
- A practical checklist for front-desk appointment coordination.
- Patient account-free booking: benefits and safeguards.
- Separating doctor and assistant access in clinic software.
- What to check before choosing dental clinic management software in India.
- Digital patient records: access, privacy and backup questions clinics should ask.
- Product release notes with real screenshots and clear change descriptions.

Each article should answer a real question, cite authoritative sources where clinical or regulatory matters are discussed, and link naturally to the relevant product page. Do not publish AI-generated medical advice as a substitute for a dentist.

## 30 / 60 / 90-day execution

### Days 1–30: foundation
- Confirm final domain, business contact details and product readiness.
- Complete the product page, contact route, privacy policy and terms.
- Fix all navigation and verify every public link in desktop and mobile browsers.
- Set up Search Console, Bing Webmaster Tools and privacy-conscious analytics.
- Publish sitemap and robots.txt after domain confirmation.

### Days 31–60: useful content
- Publish 3–4 high-quality workflow articles.
- Record 2–3 real product walkthroughs using synthetic/demo data.
- Create LinkedIn and YouTube brand profiles.
- Add product screenshots with accurate captions and alt text.
- Monitor search queries, indexing errors and page performance.

### Days 61–90: credibility and listings
- Invite a small group of real clinics to a documented pilot.
- Collect explicit permission before using clinic names, logos, screenshots or testimonials.
- Publish verified case studies only when measurable outcomes exist.
- Submit accurate software directory listings where eligibility and costs make sense.
- Improve pages using actual Search Console query and conversion data.

## Measurement

Track monthly:

- Indexed pages and search impressions/clicks.
- Relevant non-branded search queries.
- Qualified clinic enquiries.
- Demo requests and completed demos.
- Patient booking completion rate on the clinic product.
- Broken links, accessibility issues and mobile performance.

Do not promise “page one”, “top three” or a fixed number of leads. Search placement is earned over time and depends on competition, domain history, content quality, technical health and real user signals.

## Release gates

- Staging page remains noindex.
- Do not index private app/login routes.
- Do not publish unverified testimonials, customer counts, certifications, performance statistics or feature claims.
- Do not spend on ads, paid directory placements or subscriptions without explicit approval.
- Do not use real patient data in screenshots or demos.
