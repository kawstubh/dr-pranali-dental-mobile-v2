# Oralyn Signature Edition — Visual Direction

## Brand intent
A balanced premium healthcare SaaS identity: confident and established without feeling cold, welcoming to patients without looking like a consumer wellness brand, and modern enough for a professional dental-practice platform.

## Typography
- Display: Playfair Display Variable, used for marketing headlines only.
- Interface/body: Plus Jakarta Sans Variable.
- Avoid oversized display text that collides with sticky navigation; use balanced wrapping and generous line-height.

## Visual language
- Photography: authentic modern dental clinic environments and patient-care moments, with descriptive alt text, crop-safe focal points and a fallback image for the hero.
- Product visuals: clearly labelled illustrative UI previews. Never imply sample appointment details, availability, dashboards or status toggles are live clinic data.
- Surfaces: 16–28px radii, restrained borders, soft layered shadows and frosted white overlays where they improve legibility.
- Motion: small hover elevation and state transitions only; respect reduced-motion preferences.
- Layout: generous whitespace, balanced two-column hero on desktop, single-column stack below 1024px.

## Component direction
1. Sticky glass header with approved tooth icon, Oralyn wordmark and Practice OS badge.
2. Split hero with warm clinic photography, clear value proposition and dual CTAs.
3. Floating Quick Booking example card and Clinic Workspace sample card.
4. Three-pillar feature ribbon in navy/sapphire, rounded and integrated into the hero transition.
5. Product preview tabs for Doctor, Assistant and Patient experiences, explicitly labelled as illustrative.
6. Feature cards, workflow explanation, privacy/access principles and clear next steps.

## Role-specific product surfaces
- Patient: bright, welcoming white/ice-blue surfaces; simple booking; no AI diagnosis or self-diagnosis.
- Doctor: premium deep navy/sapphire workspace; clinical intelligence remains doctor-only.
- Assistant: clear lighter operational interface with separate, least-privilege access.
- Marketing palette tokens must not be used to collapse or merge the three role experiences.

## Trust and compliance guardrails
Do not publish unverified reviews, star ratings, accreditations, HIPAA claims, security certifications, patient counts, customer logos or outcome statistics. Use factual product principles until independently verified evidence is supplied.

## Release notes
- Marketing pages remain noindex while staging and before the production domain is selected.
- Keep the approved Oralyn mark unchanged; use the approved tooth-only icon with a text wordmark for the website header.
- The demo booking card and availability switch are illustrative only and must never write to production systems.
