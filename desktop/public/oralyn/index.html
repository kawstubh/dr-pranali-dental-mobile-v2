The Oralyn platform

Designed around the way a dental clinic works.

Instead of making patients and clinic teams navigate disconnected experiences, Oralyn is being shaped around the complete journey — with clear boundaries between patient-facing actions and clinical work.

↗

Patient appointment requests

A straightforward booking journey that lets patients request a visit without creating an account or remembering another password.

▦

Doctor workspace

A dedicated clinical workspace for authorised doctors to review the information and tools relevant to their practice.

◎

Clinic team workflows

Separate assistant access designed to support front-desk coordination without granting doctor-level permissions.

⌁

Connected patient journey

Bring appointment requests and clinic-side follow-up into a more consistent experience.

◇

Clear access boundaries

Keep patient-facing experiences distinct from private clinic workspaces and role-restricted information.

✦

Doctor-led intelligence

Clinical intelligence is reserved for authorised doctor workflows; it is not presented as a patient self-diagnosis tool.

Our design principles

Technology should make care feel simpler, not more complicated.

Oralyn is being developed with a practical belief: patients need a clear way to reach their clinic, and dental teams need dependable tools that respect their roles.

✓Simple patient access, without mandatory patient accounts
✓Doctor and assistant experiences with separate permissions
✓Clinical tools kept inside authorised doctor workflows
✓Responsive layouts for desktop, tablet and mobile
✓Privacy and security treated as product requirements
