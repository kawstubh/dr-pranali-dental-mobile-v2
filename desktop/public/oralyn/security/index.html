Privacy and security approach

Learn about Oralyn's role-aware access principles and privacy-first product approach. Product details remain subject to verification.

✦

Role separation

Doctor and assistant access are designed as separate roles with different permissions.

▦

Patient access

The public appointment request flow does not require a patient account, password or OTP.

◎

Clinical boundaries

Clinical intelligence is reserved for authorised doctor workflows; patients are not given AI diagnosis.

↗

Data handling

Hosting, retention, backups, encryption and operational controls must be verified against the deployed environment before any security guarantees are published.

◇

Responsible claims

Oralyn does not claim a certification, audit result or compliance status unless documented and independently verified.
