Universal Intelligence Engine

A coordinated intelligence layer, with the doctor in control.

A dentistry-focused intelligence architecture designed to connect structured dental domain knowledge with professional workflows. UIE Dentistry is intended to support the doctor experience within Oralyn, with clinical responsibility remaining firmly in the hands of the practitioner.

Doctor-facing intelligence architecture
01

Dental domain knowledge

Structured dental concepts, relationships and domain knowledge to provide a more relevant professional context.

02

Clinical reasoning support

Designed to help organise relevant clinical information and support structured professional reasoning workflows.

03

Doctor-in-the-loop safety

Professional review, judgement and decision-making remain with the qualified dental practitioner.

Clinical safety boundary

UIE Dentistry is not a patient-facing diagnosis feature. Oralyn Patient does not provide AI diagnosis or self-diagnosis. Clinical intelligence capabilities are reserved for the doctor-facing professional environment and remain subject to practitioner oversight.
